# SEO risk register

| Change | Level | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visible THIVELAB parent mark and NODELOG local label | SAFE | Keep hrefs and content routes; verify both locales |
| Product cards for existing MOA and CIPHER destinations | SAFE | Describe CIPHER as the current demo; track outbound clicks |
| LABS/ENTERPRISE marked preparing | SAFE | Non-link state until real content/service exists |
| Home hero and section order | CAUTION | Keep NODELOG article blocks and sample metadata; inspect mobile/LCP |
| Home title/description rewrite | CAUTION | Defer; visual copy alone is sufficient initially |
| Related CIPHER CTA on posts | CAUTION | Only when a real relevant challenge URL has been verified |
| Changing `WebSite`/`Organization` name, article publisher or title template | HIGH RISK | Exclude from this phase; assess search site-name impact separately |
| Article/category/series URL, slug, canonical, OG URL, redirects, locale routing | HIGH RISK | Exclude entirely; no URL migration |
| Sitemap/robots/RSS policy or removal of existing sitemap URLs | HIGH RISK | Exclude; regression gate |
| Bulk article title/description or breadcrumb schema edits | HIGH RISK | Exclude |

Existing search canonical and category `%26`/`&` differences are baseline findings. Fixing them is a separate SEO change requiring its own before/after analysis.
